How to Identify a Phishing Email

🚨 Urgent or Threatening Language

Be careful with messages that pressure you to act immediately or threaten account suspension.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Check links carefully before clicking. Unexpected or unfamiliar links can be a warning sign.

📧 Unusual Sender Address

Check whether the sender's address matches the organization or person they claim to represent.

🔐 Requests for Sensitive Information

Be cautious when an unexpected email asks for passwords, account details, or other sensitive information.

📝 Generic Greetings

Messages using generic greetings instead of your expected name or normal communication style may require additional verification.

⚠️ Unexpected Requests

Verify unusual requests through an official communication channel before taking action.

Stay Safe

When an email seems suspicious, pause and verify the message before clicking links or sharing information.
